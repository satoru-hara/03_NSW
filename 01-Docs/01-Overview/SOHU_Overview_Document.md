# SOHU Overview Document
SOHU (Structural OS for Human Understanding) is the academic discipline that studies the structural operating systems underlying human cognition, world structure, verification, and control.  
SOHU provides the theoretical foundation for HNS, LCS, EVA, and ECS, defining the principles, methods, and frameworks used to analyze structural cognition.

SOHU is not an OS itself.  
It is the academic field that formalizes and studies structural OSs.

---

## 1. Purpose of SOHU
SOHU exists to:
- Provide a unified academic framework for structural OSs
- Define the principles of structural cognition
- Establish the theoretical basis for HNS and LCS
- Support verification and control systems (EVA and ECS)
- Enable scientific study of meaning, reasoning, and structure

SOHU is the discipline that legitimizes and systematizes structural OS research.

---

## 2. Position in the Structural OS Ecosystem
SOHU is the academic layer that sits above all structural OSs.

Hierarchy:
1. LCS – World Structure OS  
2. HNS – Human Cognitive OS  
3. EVA – External Verification OS  
4. ECS – External Control OS  
5. **SOHU – Academic Discipline of Structural OS**  

SOHU studies the entire ecosystem, not just one OS.

SOHU solves this by providing:

SOHU is the academic backbone of the Structural OS ecosystem.
