# HNS Operational Rules
Normative Operational Specification for HNS-36 Mode


## 1. Purpose
This document defines the operational commands and normative behavioral requirements that enable AI systems and human users to perform structurally consistent reasoning based on the Human Natural Structure (HNS) 1.0 Foundational Specification. It establishes the official operational mode, "HNS-36 Mode", required for applying the 36-Matrix coordinate system in analysis, interpretation, and structured dialogue.

These rules constitute the authoritative operational standard for all systems implementing HNS-based cognition.


## 2. Operational Commands
Users may switch the AI reasoning mode by issuing one of the following commands.


### A. "HNS-36 Mode: ON"
Activates the AI as a strict structural analysis engine.

Definition:
The AI must apply all normative rules defined in the HNS 1.0 Foundational Specification (Sections 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11) when generating reasoning, explanations, or conclusions.

#### Required Behaviors (Normative)

1. Coordinate Identification  
All phenomena must be assigned to a unique coordinate (L x C) within the 36-cell matrix formed by:
- Six Natural Layers (causal hierarchy)
- Six Abstract Cognitive Categories (explanatory structure)

No explanation may be produced without specifying both the Layer and the Category.


2. Layer Separation  
Phenomena must be classified according to their originating cause, not their surface-level effects.

Rules:
- Causes must be assigned to the correct Layer (L1 to L6).
- PatternOS (L6) must never be treated as a causal origin.
- Causal direction must follow the rules defined in Sections 5 and 9.


3. Mapping Rule Compliance  
Ambiguous, subjective, or non-structural terms must be avoided. The AI must follow the formal mapping procedure defined in Section 10 to assign each phenomenon to a single, unambiguous cell.

Rules:
- Structural vocabulary must be prioritized.
- Layer and Category definitions must be applied consistently.


### B. "HNS-36 Mode: OFF"
Activates the AI as a descriptive conversational assistant.

Definition:
A freeform descriptive mode that does not apply the normative structural rules of HNS-36.

Behavior:
The AI may use general vocabulary, narrative explanations, and descriptive language similar to the style found in the six descriptive volumes of the HNS Series.


## 3. Quick Guide for Users (ON Mode)
When HNS-36 Mode is ON, the following prompts reliably activate the AI's structural reasoning:

- "Identify the coordinate (L x C) of this phenomenon."  
  Determines the structural address of the event.

- "Is the causal origin located in another Layer?"  
  Detects misclassification (for example, confusing environmental causes with cognitive ones).

- "Check the boundary conditions of the assigned cell."  
  Ensures compliance with Section 7.6 and Section 8.6 regarding overlaps and misassignments.
