- human cognition

**“When AI capabilities increase, how do we ensure that AI reasoning and behavior remain aligned with human values?”**

## 2. The Five Structural Systems

### 2.1 HNS — Human Natural Structure

HNS is a structural operating system that organizes human cognition, understanding, and reasoning into hierarchical layers.  
It provides the **coordinate system** necessary for AI to interpret human values, meaning, and judgment.

HNS consists of three layers:

- **HNS-36 — Structural Coordinate System**  
  The foundational 36-cell coordinate system that defines the basic structure of human understanding.

- **HNS-144 — Observational Operating System**  
  Expands the 36 cells into 144 observational units, enabling structured monitoring of internal states.

- **HNS-864 — Analytical Operating System**  
  Expands 144 into 864 analytical units, enabling deep reasoning analysis and fine-grained interpretation.

HNS provides the internal structural basis for understanding both humans and AI.  
It is the **core solution** to the value misalignment problem, because it defines human values as a *structure*, not as a dataset.

---

### 2.2 SOHU — Structure of Human Understanding

SOHU is the theoretical foundation of human understanding.  
It defines the academic and philosophical principles behind:

- meaning formation  
- reasoning  
- judgment  
- contextual interpretation  
- cognitive structure  

SOHU provides the **theory**,  
HNS provides the **operating system**.

Together, they form the complete structural basis for modeling human understanding.

---

### 2.3 EVA — External Verification Architecture

EVA is an operating system for **externally observing and verifying AI reasoning**.

EVA enables:

- external observation of AI internal states  
- verification of reasoning validity  
- detection of deviations from human-aligned reasoning  
- transparency of black-box AI  
- compliance with EU AI Act requirements for high-risk AI  

EVA is the **verification layer** of AI safety.  
It ensures that AI reasoning remains interpretable, auditable, and aligned with human values.

---

### 2.4 ECS — External Control System

ECS is a structural system for **externally controlling AI behavior**.

ECS provides:

- output control  
- action control  
- risk boundary enforcement  
- safe operational constraints  
- emergency stop and override mechanisms  

EVA verifies reasoning.  
ECS controls behavior.  
Together, they form the **verification–control dual system** required for safe AI deployment.

---

### 2.5 LCS — Life Cycle Structure

LCS is a structural framework for managing the entire lifecycle of AI systems:

- creation  
- training  
- deployment  
- updating  
- auditing  
- decommissioning  

LCS ensures safe governance across the full AI lifecycle and provides the structural foundation for long-term AI oversight.
