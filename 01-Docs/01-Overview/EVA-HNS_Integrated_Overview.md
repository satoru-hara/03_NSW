## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document provides an integrated overview of the five structural systems that form the EVA-HNS Full-Stack Architecture:

- HNS (Human Natural Structure)
- SOHU (Structure of Human Understanding)
- EVA (External Verification Architecture)
- ECS (External Control System)
- LCS (Life Cycle Structure)

These systems together define the world's first **structural full-stack operating system** for human cognition and AI safety.

## 2. The Five Structural Systems

### 2.1 HNS — Human Natural Structure  
A structural operating system that organizes human cognition, understanding, and reasoning into hierarchical layers:

- **HNS-36** — Structural Coordinate System  
- **HNS-144** — Observational Operating System  
- **HNS-864** — Analytical Operating System  

HNS provides the internal structural basis for understanding both humans and AI.

---

### 2.2 SOHU — Structure of Human Understanding  
The theoretical foundation of human understanding.  
SOHU defines the academic and philosophical principles behind:

- meaning formation  
- reasoning  
- judgment  
- contextual interpretation  
- cognitive structure  

SOHU = theory  
HNS = operating system

---

### 2.3 EVA — External Verification Architecture  
An operating system for **externally observing and verifying AI reasoning**.

EVA enables:

- external observation of AI internal states  
- verification of reasoning validity  
- transparency of black-box AI  
- compliance with EU AI Act requirements for high-risk AI  

EVA is the core of AI safety.

---

### 2.4 ECS — External Control System  
A structural system for **externally controlling AI behavior**.

ECS provides:

- output control  
- action control  
- risk boundary enforcement  
- safe operational constraints  

EVA = verification  
ECS = control

---

### 2.5 LCS — Life Cycle Structure  
A structural framework for managing the entire lifecycle of AI systems:

- creation  
- training  
- deployment  
- updating  
- auditing  
- decommissioning  

LCS ensures safe governance across the full AI lifecycle.
