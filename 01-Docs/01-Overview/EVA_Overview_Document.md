# EVA Overview Document
EVA (External Verification Architecture) is a structural operating system designed to externally verify the reasoning, outputs, and internal consistency of AI systems.  
EVA provides an independent verification layer that AI models cannot modify or override, ensuring stable, interpretable, and trustworthy behavior.

EVA is not a model, dataset, or training method.  
It is a structural OS that defines how AI reasoning is validated from outside the model.

---

## 1. Purpose of EVA
EVA exists to provide:
- External verification of AI reasoning
- Structural guarantees of interpretive stability
- Protection against model drift and hallucination
- A consistent method for evaluating AI outputs
- A bridge between human cognition (HNS) and AI behavior

EVA ensures that AI systems remain aligned with human-defined structures.

---

## 2. Position in the Structural OS Ecosystem
EVA is positioned between:
- HNS (Human Natural Structure) — the human cognitive OS  
- ECS (External Control System) — the AI behavior control OS  

Hierarchy:
1. LCS – World Structure OS  
2. HNS – Human Cognitive OS  
3. **EVA – External Verification OS**  
4. ECS – External Control OS  

EVA depends on HNS for meaning and structural reference.

---

## 3. Core Functions of EVA
### 3.1 Reasoning Verification
EVA checks whether AI reasoning:
- Follows natural structure  
- Aligns with human cognition  
- Maintains semantic invariance  
- Avoids hallucination or drift  

### 3.2 Structural Consistency Checking
EVA evaluates:
- Layer alignment (LCS)  
- Cognitive alignment (HNS)  
- Semantic coherence  
- Causal correctness  

### 3.3 Immutable External Layer
EVA operates outside the AI model:
- AI cannot modify EVA  
- AI cannot bypass EVA  
- AI cannot influence verification rules  

This ensures independence and stability.

---

## 4. Relationship to HNS
HNS provides the reference baseline for EVA.

HNS defines:
- Human meaning structure  
- Natural reasoning patterns  
- Cognitive invariants  
- Interpretive coordinates  

EVA verifies AI by comparing it to **HNS-based structure**.

Thus:
- HNS = reference OS  
- EVA = verification OS  

This relationship is central to the licensing value of HNS.

---

## 5. Relationship to LCS
LCS provides the world-level structure that HNS inherits.  
EVA uses LCS to verify:
- Layer correctness  
- Causal ordering  
- Structural grounding  

EVA does not replace LCS; it uses it as a coordinate system.

---

## 6. Relationship to ECS
EVA verifies reasoning.  
ECS governs behavior.

Flow:
1. AI produces reasoning  
2. EVA verifies reasoning  
3. ECS determines allowed actions  

EVA → ECS is a verification-control pipeline.

---

## 7. Why EVA Is Needed
Modern AI systems:
- Hallucinate  
- Drift  
- Lose context  
- Produce unstable reasoning  
- Cannot self-verify  

EVA solves this by providing:
- External structure  
- External verification  
- External invariance  

This is impossible for AI to achieve internally.

---

## 8. Status of EVA
EVA is:
- A structural OS  
- Independent of model architecture  
- Compatible with any AI system  
- Dependent on HNS as its reference baseline  
- Positioned for future standardization  

EVA is not a product; it is a structural requirement.
