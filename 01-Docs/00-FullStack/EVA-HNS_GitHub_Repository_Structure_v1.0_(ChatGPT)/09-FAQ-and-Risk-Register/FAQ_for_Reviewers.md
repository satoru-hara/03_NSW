# FAQ for Reviewers

## Does EVA-HNS claim to eliminate all hallucinations?

No. The safest demonstrated claim is that EVA-HNS can produce coordinate-based, externally auditable verification records.

## Does EVA-HNS require changing model weights?

No for post-hoc auditing. Pre-decode intervention would require deeper integration.

## Is EVA-HNS an official standard?

No. It is an author-led research and demonstration framework.

## What is the strongest current claim?

Auditability: each evaluation can be traced to HNS coordinates, SMS-6 grounding layers, failure classifications, and EVA verdicts.

## What is the next necessary step?

Independent third-party validation across models, languages, tasks, and evaluators.
