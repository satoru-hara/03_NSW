# 2. What HNS Solves (Why It Exists)

Modern AI systems lack:
- stable internal meaning structure
- cross-context coherence
- explicit reasoning coordinates
- reproducible meaning representation

Humans naturally use structured meaning:
- goals, roles, values, constraints, narratives

**HNS provides the missing structural layer that aligns human meaning and AI cognition.**

---

# 3. Core Principles of HNS

1. **Universality**  
   One structure for humans, organizations, and AI.

2. **Reproducibility**  
   Same input -> same structural representation.

3. **Explainability**  
   Reasoning paths become explicit and inspectable.

4. **Model Independence**  
   Works with any AI model or architecture.

5. **Safety and Governance**  
   Structural constraints prevent inconsistency and hallucination.
