1. COPYRIGHT OWNERSHIP
All copyrights and IP related to HNS remain the exclusive property
of Satoru Hara. This includes all conceptual structures, diagrams,
coordinate systems, architectural models, and documentation.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. LICENSE REQUIREMENT FOR IMPLEMENTATIONS
Any development or deployment of HNS-based systems requires a paid
license agreement with Satoru Hara. No party may implement HNS
Runtime, EVA, SOHU, or derivative structures without such a license.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. ACCEPTANCE OF MINOR REVISIONS FOR STANDARDIZATION
Minor technical or editorial revisions may be required by ISO/IEC,
CEN/CENELEC, JISC, NIST, or other bodies. All copyrights and
derivative works created during standardization remain with
Satoru Hara.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. SALE OF PERPETUAL, ROYALTY-FREE LICENSE TO FOUNDATION
A perpetual, royalty-free usage license may be sold exclusively to
the Human Natural Structure Foundation. This license
is limited to non-commercial use, dissemination, standardization
support, and governance activities. All copyrights remain with
Satoru Hara.

------------------------------------------------------------

5. POST-FOUNDATION COMMERCIAL LICENSING MODEL
After receiving the perpetual license, the Foundation becomes the
global governance and licensing body for HNS. Commercial entities
must obtain paid licenses from the Foundation. Licensing revenue
supports the Foundation’s mission and global dissemination. All IP
continues to belong to Satoru Hara.
