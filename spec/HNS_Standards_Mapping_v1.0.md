# HNS–Standards Mapping
# Version: 1.0
# Author: Satoru Hara

This document maps the Human Natural Structure (HNS) framework to major
international AI standards and regulatory frameworks.  
HNS functions as a structural OS that provides the foundational coordinate
system beneath these standards.  
It does not replace existing standards; it enables consistent interpretation,
implementation, and verification across them.

# 3. Structural Role of HNS in Standards Alignment

HNS contributes to international standards in four key ways:

1. **Structural Grounding**  
   Provides a unified coordinate system (36 cells) for interpreting human meaning,
   enabling consistent application of standards across domains.

2. **Contextual Coherence**  
   Ensures that physical, emotional, intentional, relational, and societal factors
   are not mixed or misinterpreted.

3. **Interpretability and Traceability**  
   Each AI decision can be mapped to a specific HNS cell, enabling transparent
   reasoning aligned with ISO/IEEE/NIST requirements.

4. **Safety and Risk Reduction**  
   Structural flows (vertical and horizontal) provide predictable transitions,
   reducing hallucinations and misalignment.

and implementable across diverse AI systems.

HNS is the missing foundational layer that unifies:

- management (ISO 42001)

This mapping enables global standardization and consistent implementation.
