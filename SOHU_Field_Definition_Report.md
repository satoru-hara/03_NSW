## Abstract

This report proposes Structural Operating Systems for Human Understanding (SOHU) as a new academic field. SOHU studies the structural operating system layer for human understanding, treating the natural, cognitive, social, and civilizational structures of humans as an integrated OS. Its foundational implementation is the Human Natural Structure (HNS) framework — specifically the HNS-36 coordinate system — which provides a unified structural reference shared by AI, individuals, organizations, and civilizations. SOHU formalizes structural infrastructure for human understanding that has not existed within psychology, cognitive science, AI engineering, or OS engineering. This report defines the field, establishes its core architecture, and articulates its research agenda.

## 1. Field Declaration: Definition of SOHU

SOHU (Structural Operating Systems for Human Understanding) is a new academic field dedicated to studying the structural operating system layer for human understanding. The field treats the natural, cognitive, social, and civilizational structures of humans as an integrated OS, providing a unified structural coordinate system shared by AI, individuals, organizations, societies, and civilizations.

SOHU formalizes the structural infrastructure for human understanding that has never existed within psychology, cognitive science, AI engineering, or OS engineering. Its foundational proposition is that human understanding has a discoverable structural architecture — one that can be formalized, specified, and made operable as an operating system for both human and artificial agents.

---

## 2. Background: Why SOHU Is Needed

The emergence of SOHU is driven by a convergence of structural absences across disciplines:

- AI is shifting from capability-focused development to cognition alignment — yet no structural OS layer for human understanding exists to ground this alignment.
- Psychology and cognitive science describe phenomena but lack structural coordinate models.
- AI engineering lacks a coordinate system for human cognitive structure.
- OS engineering has historically been limited to computational systems.
- Semantic grounding remains a major unresolved limitation in AI systems.
- Structural understanding of society and civilization remains fragmented across disconnected disciplines.

SOHU emerges as the necessary field to fill these structural gaps — not by extending any existing discipline, but by formalizing a new OS-level layer that all existing disciplines presuppose but none has defined.

## 3. Core Concept: Structural Operating Systems

### 3.1 What Is a Structural OS?

A structural OS is an operating system for human understanding. It provides hierarchical, abstract, and interoperable structures for modeling humans and societies — analogous to the role a computational OS plays for hardware and software, but applied to the domain of human meaning and cognition.

### 3.2 OS Characteristics of SOHU

SOHU-compliant structural OS frameworks are defined by the following properties:

- Abstraction — separates structural description from content or cultural specificity  
- Layering — organizes structures in a causal hierarchy from physical to civilizational  
- Specification — formal, referenceable definitions operable by both humans and AI  
- Extensibility — accommodates new domains without modifying core architecture  
- Backward compatibility — prior specifications remain valid under extension  
- Context integration — embeds contextual and meaning-level information within the structural framework  

### 3.3 Distinction from Existing Models

Existing frameworks address partial dimensions of human understanding:

- Psychology → phenomenon-based: describes without structural formalization  
- Cognitive science → process-based: models processing without coordinate systems  
- AI engineering → computation-based: optimizes without structural human grounding  
- OS engineering → machine-based: formalizes computation, not human meaning  

SOHU introduces the first OS layer specifically for human understanding — an integrated structural framework that is neither reducible to nor derivable from any of the above.

## 4. HNS: The Central OS Implementation of SOHU

The Human Natural Structure (HNS) is the core OS implementation of SOHU. It provides the foundational coordinate system through which the SOHU field operates.

HNS is structured around:

- Six Natural Layers (causal structure): PhysicalOS · CognitiveOS · InteractionOS · EnvironmentOS · LoadOS · PatternOS  
- Six Abstract Cognitive Categories (explanatory structure)  
- Coordinate systems: HNS-36 · HNS-144 · HNS-864  
- Unified structural coverage of individuals, societies, and civilizations  
- Foundation for Semantic OS and Civilization Framework  

HNS-36 — the 36-cell matrix formed by the intersection of six natural layers and six cognitive categories — serves as the canonical reference coordinate system: the stable, closed specification from which all HNS extensions and applications derive.


## 6. Coordinate Systems: HNS-36 / HNS-144 / HNS-864

### 6.1 HNS-36

The foundational coordinate system. A 36-cell matrix combining six natural layers and six cognitive categories. HNS-36 defines the minimal structure sufficient to locate any unit of human meaning within the coordinate space. It is formally closed and functions as the stable reference frame for all higher-resolution extensions.

### 6.2 HNS-144

An expanded coordinate system incorporating environment, load, and pattern dimensions at increased resolution. HNS-144 supports analysis of contexts requiring finer structural differentiation while remaining backward-compatible with HNS-36.

### 6.3 HNS-864

A high-resolution coordinate system designed for societal and civilizational structural analysis. HNS-864 extends the coordinate space to accommodate the full complexity of collective human structures without sacrificing the hierarchical integrity established in HNS-36.
