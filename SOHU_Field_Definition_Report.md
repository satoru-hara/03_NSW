# The SOHU Field: Structural Operating Systems for Human Understanding

## 1. Field Declaration: Definition of SOHU
SOHU is a new academic field that studies the structural operating system layer for human understanding.  
This field treats the natural, cognitive, social, and civilizational structures of humans as an integrated OS,  
providing a unified structural coordinate system shared by AI, individuals, organizations, societies, and civilizations.  
SOHU formalizes the structural infrastructure for human understanding that has never existed in psychology,  
cognitive science, AI engineering, or OS engineering.

---

## 2. Background: Why SOHU Is Needed
- AI is shifting from capability-focused development to cognition alignment.  
- The OS layer for human understanding has been historically absent.  
- Psychology and cognitive science describe phenomena but lack structural models.  
- AI engineering lacks a coordinate system for human structure.  
- OS engineering has been limited to computational systems.  
- Semantic grounding remains a major limitation in AI.  
- Structural understanding of society and civilization has been fragmented.

SOHU emerges as the necessary field to fill these structural gaps.
