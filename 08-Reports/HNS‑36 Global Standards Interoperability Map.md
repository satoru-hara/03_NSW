# HNS‑36 Global Standards Interoperability Map  
### Human Natural Structure × International AI Standards  
### (ISO/IEC 42001 • NIST AI RMF • EU AI Act)

This document provides the interoperability mapping between the **HNS‑36 coordinate system**  
(6 Human Natural Layers × 6 Abstract Cognitive Categories)  
and the major global AI governance standards.

HNS‑36 functions as a **structural OS** that positions any phenomenon at the intersection of  
**causal origin (Layer)** × **explanatory purpose (Category)**.  
This map demonstrates how HNS‑36 provides the internal structure required for compliance with  
international AI standards.

---

## 1. HNS‑36 Overview

### Human Natural Layers (Causal Structure)
- **L1 PhysicalOS** — physical & biological foundations  
- **L2 CognitiveOS** — perception, memory, internal processing  
- **L3 InteractionOS** — communication, cooperation, behavior  
- **L4 EnvironmentOS** — physical, social, institutional environments  
- **L5 LoadOS** — internal/external loads, pressures, constraints  
- **L6 PatternOS** — emergent outcomes, norms, long-term patterns  

### Abstract Cognitive Categories (Non‑causal Explanatory Axes)
- **C1 Civilization** — macro-scale, historical, civilizational framing  
- **C2 Core** — essential structures, foundational principles  
- **C3 Module** — components, elements, functional units  
- **C4 Application** — practical use, applied context  
- **C5 System** — organized mechanisms, institutional processes  
- **C6 External** — contextual factors, external influences

## 5. Why HNS‑36 Enables Global Interoperability

HNS‑36 satisfies the structural needs of all major AI standards because:

- Layers define **where** a phenomenon originates (causal domain).  
- Categories define **how** it is interpreted (explanatory axis).  
- Cells define **structural positions**, not content.  
- The 36‑Matrix provides a **universal coordinate system** for AI governance, risk, and compliance.

This makes HNS‑36 a **de facto internal OS** for aligning AI systems with global regulatory  
requirements.
