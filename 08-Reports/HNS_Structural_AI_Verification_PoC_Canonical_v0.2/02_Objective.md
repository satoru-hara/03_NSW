# 2. PoC Objective

The objective of the HNS Structural AI Verification PoC is to demonstrate that
AI-generated reasoning can be externally evaluated and stabilized using the canonical
HNS-36 coordinate system.

The PoC aims to prove four core capabilities:

## 2.1 Structural Classification of AI Reasoning
HNS can map each AI-generated claim into a fixed structural coordinate:

- Human Natural Layer (L1–L6)
- Abstract Cognitive Category (C1–C6)

This mapping makes the structure of reasoning visible and auditable.

## 2.2 Detection of Structural Inconsistency
The PoC identifies when an AI answer violates structural boundaries, including:

- Layer jumps  
- Category ambiguity  
- Unsupported causal claims  
- Scope drift  
- External facts treated as internal states  

These violations indicate structural instability in the reasoning process.

## 2.3 Generation of a Verification Report
The system produces a structured verification report that includes:

- Detected structural issues  
- Coordinate mapping  
- Stability score  
- Recommended corrections  

This creates an external audit trail for AI reasoning.

## 2.4 Model-Independent AI Safety Support
The verification layer operates outside the AI model.  
It does not require:

- access to training data  
- internal model weights  
- vendor-specific evaluation tools  

This makes HNS suitable for:

- standards-based auditing  
- enterprise governance  
- third-party evaluation  
- cross-model comparison  

The PoC therefore demonstrates that structural verification can improve AI safety
without modifying or accessing the underlying model.
