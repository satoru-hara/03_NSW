# 14. Future Work

The HNS Structural AI Verification PoC demonstrates that external, model-independent
reasoning verification is feasible.  
However, the prototype represents only the first step toward a full verification
architecture suitable for standardization and large-scale deployment.

Future work focuses on four major directions:

1. Expanded Mapping Logic  
2. Advanced Structural Verification  
3. Integration with EVA / SOHU  
4. Standardization and Industry Adoption  

---

# 14.1 Expanded Mapping Logic

Future versions of the PoC will include:

### 1. More granular subject classification  
Distinguish between:
- individual humans  
- groups  
- institutions  
- biological subsystems  
- artificial systems  

### 2. Verb-class expansion  
Improve mapping for:
- emotional verbs  
- epistemic verbs  
- normative verbs  
- multi-agent verbs  

### 3. Context-aware mapping  
Use local context to refine coordinate assignment.

### 4. Multi-claim relational mapping  
Detect when claims form a structured chain (e.g., escalation, mitigation).

---

# 14.2 Advanced Structural Verification

Future improvements include:

### 1. Cross-claim contradiction detection  
Identify when two claims cannot both be true structurally.

### 2. Causal chain validation  
Check whether transitions between coordinates follow valid causal patterns.

### 3. Normative reasoning detection  
Identify when the model introduces:
- moral judgments  
- obligations  
- expectations  
without grounding.

### 4. Multi-layer drift detection  
Detect when reasoning drifts across layers without justification.

### 5. Severity-weighted violation taxonomy  
Differentiate between:
- minor inconsistencies  
- major structural failures  

---

# 14.3 Integration with EVA / SOHU

The PoC is designed to evolve into a component of:

### 1. EVA (External Verification Architecture)  
HNS-36 becomes the structural verification layer for:
- reasoning audits  
- safety checks  
- governance pipelines  

### 2. SOHU (Structural Operating Human Unit)  
HNS-36 coordinates become part of:
- human-AI interaction models  
- structural OS-level reasoning modules  

### 3. Full-stack HNS integration  
Future versions will integrate with:
- HNS-36  
- HNS-72  
- HNS-144  
depending on required granularity.

---

# 14.4 Standardization and Industry Adoption

Future work includes:

### 1. ISO/IEC standardization proposals  
Formalizing:
- HNS-36 coordinate definitions  
- structural violation taxonomy  
- verification pipeline  

### 2. Industry reference implementation  
Providing:
- open-source libraries  
- test suites  
- compliance benchmarks  

### 3. Cross-model evaluation  
Applying HNS-36 to:
- LLMs  
- multimodal models  
- agentic systems  

### 4. Governance integration  
Embedding structural verification into:
- enterprise AI governance  
- safety audits  
- regulatory compliance  

---

# 14.5 Research Directions

### 1. Structural coherence vs factual accuracy  
Study how structural stability correlates with truthfulness.

### 2. Human interpretability  
Evaluate how easily humans can understand coordinate-based reasoning.

### 3. Multi-agent structural analysis  
Extend HNS-36 to conversations between multiple AI systems.

### 4. Long-form reasoning  
Adapt the PoC to handle:
- long documents  
- multi-paragraph arguments  
- multi-step reasoning chains  

---
