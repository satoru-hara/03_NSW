# LCS（Large Cognitive Structure）

## Overview
LCS is a large‑scale cognitive structure framework designed to unify human and AI cognition, meaning, and reasoning within a single structural system.  
Unlike Large Language Models (LLMs), which operate on statistical patterns of language and knowledge, LCS explicitly defines the deeper causal layers, semantic categories, and relational structures that constitute cognition itself.

By providing a formal **cognitive coordinate system**, LCS ensures structural consistency, interpretive stability, and coherent reasoning across diverse contexts.  
This reduces the risk of misalignment or misunderstanding between humans and AI.

LCS is positioned as a foundational concept for next‑generation AI safety, cognitive alignment, and structural reasoning, offering a potential standard for how AI systems interpret and interact with human cognitive structures.

# HNS（Human Natural Structure）

## Overview
HNS is a **Natural Cognitive OS** that organizes human cognition, meaning, and behavior into a 36‑cell structural system, defined through the combination of causal layers and explanatory categories.  
This 36‑cell framework functions as a precise cognitive coordinate system, enabling AI systems to interpret human intentions, contexts, and meanings without distortion or misalignment.

As the only cognitive specification grounded in natural human structure, HNS provides a structural foundation for suppressing AI hallucinations, maintaining reasoning consistency, and achieving human–AI cognitive alignment.

HNS also serves as the **first implementation of the LCS (Large Cognitive Structure)** category, functioning as a **Pre‑Standard Specification** for future cognitive‑structure standards.

It offers a unifying structural basis that spans psychology, sociology, behavioral science, and AI engineering, enabling cross‑domain integration through a single natural cognitive framework.

HNS was proposed in **2026 by S. Hara** and is increasingly recognized as a new foundational model for human understanding in the age of advanced AI.
