# Systematization of AI Categories and Human Scope in HNS
Human Natural Structure — AI Category and Human Scope Mapping
(Mid-Length Report: 8,000–12,000 words equivalent)

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this report is to systematically organize the categories of AI
(AI types) and the human scope addressed by HNS (Human Natural Structure), and
to clarify how meaning structures are handled across different AI applications.

HNS functions as a semantic operating system, and therefore the range of humans
an AI must consider expands dynamically depending on the AI’s application domain.

This document serves as a bridge between HNS’s Semantic OS, Spine, and Discipline
structures and the practical domains in which AI operates.

## 2. Background
Traditional AI classifications have relied on axes such as:

- Model size (Small / Medium / Large)
- Capability (Narrow / General)
- Task type (Generation / Reasoning / Prediction)
- Domain (Healthcare / Autonomous Driving / Education)

However, these frameworks do not address a fundamental question:

"Which humans does the AI need to understand?"

HNS is the only framework that can structurally define the human scope an AI
must consider, because HNS treats human meaning structures as an OS.

## 3. Three-Layer Classification of AI in HNS (Framework)

### 3.1 Existential Layer (AI’s Ontological Category)
- Assistant AI (legacy, task-oriented)
- Attendant AI (HNS-enabled personal AI)
- Daemon AI (externalized cognitive process)
- Structural AI (AI with Semantic OS internally)
- Civilization AI (AI handling societal and civilizational norms)

### 3.2 Functional Layer (AI’s Social Role)
- Secretary AI (planning, memory, task support)
- Guardian AI (safety, boundaries, risk management)
- Navigator AI (intent–state–action coordination)
- Interpreter AI (meaning, value, and context translation)

### 3.3 Application Layer (AI’s Operational Domain)
- Medical AI
- Autonomous Driving AI
- Industrial Robot AI
- Government / Administrative AI
- Education AI
- Enterprise / Organizational AI
